DNA Marker Explorer

Open-source, offline. Compares your raw DNA file with ~70 well-studied markers. Each marker has an evidence grade, an ancestry-fit badge and PubMed references.

🔒 Your file never leaves this computer. It's read in your browser's memory. The page can't send anything anywhere: its Content-Security-Policy blocks every network request, and it has no server, analytics or external files. Closing the tab clears everything.

How to read this, and its limits
  • Grade. A clinical guideline (CPIC/DPWG) or an established single-gene effect. B genome-wide significant and replicated, but a modest effect. C single study, candidate gene, or a small or inconsistent effect. Full rules: docs/EVIDENCE.md.
  • Ancestry fit. ✓ replicated the association has been replicated in your ancestry group. ~ biology general not studied specifically, but the mechanism (e.g. enzyme function) applies to everyone. ? unknown found mostly in other populations; the effect in yours may be smaller, larger or absent. See docs/ANCESTRY.md.
  • Most risk variants shift odds slightly. An odds ratio of 1.3 on a 10% baseline risk means about 12.5%. Lifestyle and family history usually matter more.
  • Arrays miss things. Rare alleles, deletions and duplications (important for CYP2D6) aren't detected, so "typical" isn't a guarantee.
  • Deliberately excluded: BRCA1/2 and other rare disease mutations (consumer arrays give frequent false positives) and polygenic scores (poor accuracy outside European ancestry).
  • Not medical advice. Confirm anything actionable with a clinical test and a doctor or genetic counsellor.
